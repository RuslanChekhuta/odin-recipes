Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made of stacked layers of flat pasta alternated with various fillings and baked in an oven. Originating in Italy, this traditional comfort food has become popular worldwide, often served in large casseroles as a hearty family meal. The name itself refers to both the wide, rectangular sheets of pasta and the final culinary creation, which embodies the warmth and richness of authentic Italian cooking.

The flavor of lasagna is deeply savory, comforting, and complex, blending the tanginess of tomato sauce with the rich creaminess of cheese and meat. Standard ingredients include wide lasagna noodles, a robust ragù (ground meat cooked with tomatoes, onions, and herbs), and a luscious cheese mixture typically featuring ricotta, mozzarella, and Parmesan—or a traditional European Béchamel sauce. Baked until bubbling and golden-brown on top, the magic of lasagna lies in how the edges turn delightfully crispy while the center remains incredibly soft, melted, and flavorful.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets (or lasagna noodles)
  • Ground beef (or a mix of beef and pork)
  • Crushed tomatoes (or tomato purée)
  • Tomato paste
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Ricotta cheese (or Béchamel sauce)
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Fresh basil
  • Dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper

Steps

  1. Prepare the meat sauce — Sauté onions and garlic in olive oil, add the ground meat, brown it completely, then stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and herbs, and let it simmer.
  2. Make the Béchamel sauce — Melt the butter in a saucepan, stir in the flour to create a paste (roux), then gradually whisk in the milk and cook until thick, seasoning with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg.
  3. Boil the pasta sheets — Cook the lasagna noodles in boiling salted water until al dente, then drain them, or skip this step if using no-boil sheets.
  4. Preheat the oven — Set your oven to 190°C (375°F) so it reaches the right temperature before baking.
  5. Assemble the layers — Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish, then alternate layers of pasta sheets, Béchamel sauce, meat sauce, and shredded mozzarella.
  6. Top with cheese — Cover the final layer of pasta with the remaining sauce, a generous amount of mozzarella, and grated Parmesan.
  7. Bake the lasagna — Cover the dish with foil and bake for 25 minutes, then remove the foil and bake for another 15–20 minutes until the top is bubbling and golden-brown.
  8. Rest and serve — Let the lasagna rest for 10–15 minutes after baking to allow the layers to set before slicing.
